# Day-trip blossom venues held outside V1

Checked 21 August 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

These venues have strong first-party blossom and visitor evidence, but CherryMap V1 is limited to the Greater Sydney launch map and explicitly defers day-trip destinations. They must not be copied into `data/locations.geojson` unless the product boundary is changed through review.

| Venue | Official evidence | Conservative future record | V1 decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blue Mountains Botanic Garden Mount Tomah — Residence Garden | [Botanic Gardens of Sydney](https://www.botanicgardens.org.au/blue-mountains-botanic-garden-mount-tomah/garden-highlights/ornamental-gardens) says cherry blossoms cover the Residence Garden lawn in spring; [visitor information](https://www.botanicgardens.org.au/blue-mountains-botanic-garden-mount-tomah/plan-your-visit) provides current access details. | One public venue-level flowering-cherry pin; species unpublished. | Hold for the later day-trip collection. |
| Wildwood Garden — Weeping Cherry Walk, Bilpin | The venue's [history](https://www.wildwoodgarden.com.au/history/) identifies nine `Prunus subhirtella 'Rosea'`; its [2026 opening page](https://www.wildwoodgarden.com.au/opening-details/) provides seasonal ticketed access. | One ticketed venue-level flowering-cherry pin; no photo reuse. | Hold for the later day-trip collection. |
| Everglades House & Gardens — Cherry Terrace, Leura | The [National Trust venue page](https://www.nationaltrust.org.au/places/everglades-house-gardens-2/) confirms September cherry blossoms, current ticketed access and the public address. | One ticketed venue-level flowering-cherry pin; no photo reuse. | Hold for the later day-trip collection and outside the current longitude gate. |

The evidence supports venue/group records only. It does not justify individual-tree coordinates, current bloom claims, or reuse of source media.
